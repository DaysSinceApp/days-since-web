Days Since Privacy Policy (Android)

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Days Since for Android is developed by A Couple of Friends OOD. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at android [at] dayssinceapp [dot] com.

Local App Data

Days Since does not require an account.

The counters, names, dates, notes, settings, and other content you enter in the app are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this content unless you choose to send it to us, for example in a support email or screenshot.

Crash Reporting and Analytics

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to help us find and fix crashes and stability issues. Crashlytics may collect crash logs, diagnostic information, app version, device model, Android version, app state at the time of a crash, session information, and installation identifiers.

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Analytics may collect app interactions, feature usage, session information, app version, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from IP address, app-instance identifiers, and purchase or subscription events.

We do not use Google Analytics to collect the content of your counters, notes, or dates. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics.

We do not show ads, and we do not use Google Analytics for advertising, ads personalization, remarketing, Google Signals, Google Ads linking, or user-provided data collection. We disable Advertising ID collection for Google Analytics.

Analytics and crash reporting are enabled by default. You can turn them off in the app's Settings screen.

Google explains how it uses information from apps that use its services here.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Days Since offers optional paid features, trials, or subscriptions through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your credit card number, bank account details, or other payment card information.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases, trials, subscriptions, subscription status, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, Google purchase tokens, anonymous app user identifiers, device or operating system information, and related transaction information.

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy is available here.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, message, screenshots, and any other details you include.

We use support emails only to respond to you, provide support, troubleshoot issues, and maintain support records. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the last exchange, unless we need to keep them longer for refunds, disputes, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal requirements.

Sharing, Security, and Deletion

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We share limited information with service providers that help us operate the app, including Google/Firebase, Google Play, and RevenueCat. We may also disclose information where legally required.

Information sent from the app to our service providers is transmitted using encrypted connections.

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

You can contact us at android [at] dayssinceapp [dot] com to request deletion of information we can reasonably identify and control.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact us at android [at] dayssinceapp [dot] com.

Children

Days Since is intended for users aged 13 and older. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it where required.
