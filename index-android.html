Days Since Privacy Policy (Android)

Last updated: February 2, 2026

Overview

We take your privacy very seriously. The Days Since Android app does not require an account and does not collect personal data that directly identifies you (such as your name, email address, or phone number). All information that you input into the Days Since app remains stored locally on your device.

Crash reporting & Analytics

To help us improve stability and understand how the app is used, the Android app uses the following Google services:

Firebase Crashlytics

Days Since uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting. This helps us identify crashes and stability issues (for example, device model, Android version, and the app state at the time of the crash) so we can fix bugs faster.

Google Analytics

Days Since uses Google Analytics to better understand how features are used (for example, how many counters users create on average, how many users use Dark Mode, etc.). This information is used to improve the app and prioritize development work.

The information collected through these services is used for app improvement and is not sold or rented to third parties.

Payment Data and Purchase History

The App uses RevenueCat to manage trial versions and paid subscriptions. RevenueCat's Privacy Policy can be found here.

Your Consent

By using the Days Since app, you consent to this privacy policy.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
