Days Since Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

We take your privacy very seriously. Days Since app does not collect any personal data. All information that you input into the Days Since app remains privately stored on your device.

Days Since is developed by A Couple of Friends OOD. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact [at] dayssinceapp [dot] com.

Local App Data

Days Since does not require an account.

The counters, names, dates, notes, settings, and other content you enter in the app are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this content unless you choose to send it to us, for example in a support email or screenshot.

Crash Reporting and Analytics

We use Sentry for crash reporting. Sentry helps us find and fix crashes and stability issues. Sentry may collect crash logs, diagnostic information, app version, device model, iOS version, app state at the time of a crash, session information, and installation or device identifiers.

We use a self-hosted instance of Countly for analytics. Countly helps us understand how the app is used and improve it. Countly may collect app interactions, feature usage, session information, app version, device model, iOS version, country-level approximate location, and a device or app identifier.

We do not send names, email addresses, counter names, notes, dates, or custom user IDs to Sentry or Countly.

You can turn crash reporting and analytics on or off in the app's Settings tab.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Days Since offers optional paid features, trials, or subscriptions through Apple's App Store.

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive your credit card number, bank account details, Apple ID, or other payment card information.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases, trials, subscriptions, subscription status, and purchase restoration. RevenueCat may process purchase history, subscription status, Apple receipts, anonymous app user identifiers, device or operating system information, and related transaction information.

RevenueCat's Privacy Policy is available here.

Sharing Usage Data with Apple

When a user requests a subscription refund through Apple, we may share limited usage data with Apple to help assess whether the subscription was actively used. This data may include anonymized feature usage or engagement patterns (e.g., how often the app was opened or used), and is shared only when a refund is requested.

This information does not include personal identifiers such as name, email, or Apple ID, and is used only for refund evaluation purposes. The data is transmitted securely and handled in compliance with Apple’s and our privacy obligations.

Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name, message, screenshots, and any other details you include.

We use support emails only to respond to you, provide support, troubleshoot issues, and maintain support records. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the last exchange, unless we need to keep them longer for refunds, disputes, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal requirements.

Sharing, Security, and Deletion

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We share limited information with service providers that help us operate the app, including Sentry, Countly, Apple, and RevenueCat. We may also disclose information where legally required.

Information sent from the app to our service providers is transmitted using encrypted connections.

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

You can contact us at contact [at] dayssinceapp [dot] com to request deletion of information we can reasonably identify and control.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a privacy request, contact us at contact [at] dayssinceapp [dot] com.

Children

Days Since is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it where required.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and will be effective when posted unless stated otherwise.
